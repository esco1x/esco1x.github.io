Relay / About

A cleaner home for launcher accounts.

Relay gives you one consistent desktop interface for the launcher accounts you choose to save on your Windows PC.

How switching works

Clear and local

1. Sign in normally

Open the platform’s own launcher and complete its official sign-in, two-factor, passkey or security prompt.

2. Save the profile

Relay reads the supported local sign-in selection or session information and stores it encrypted for your Windows user.

3. Choose it later

When you switch, Relay applies the launcher-specific local selection and opens the launcher again.

Designed for the desktop

Not a browser wrapper

Launcher-specific UI

Each switcher uses its own look and behaviour instead of forcing every client into one generic screen.

Names and images

Customise how saved profiles appear in Relay without changing public launcher identities.

Main settings

Performance mode, Discord Rich Presence and installation controls live in Relay’s main home settings.
