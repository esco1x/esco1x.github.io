Relay / Privacy

Local profiles. Official sign-in.

Relay is designed around clear boundaries: local profile management belongs in Relay; passwords and account verification stay with the platforms that own them.

What Relay does

On this Windows PC

Encrypted local storage

Relay profile data and recovery information are encrypted for the current Windows user.

Supported local changes

Relay updates only the local launcher settings, remembered choices or session material required by its respective switcher.

Optional Discord presence

When enabled, Discord can show the selected launcher and profile count. Account names and sign-in material are never shown.

What Relay does not do

Kept with the platform

Collect passwords

Relay does not ask you to type launcher passwords, authenticator codes, passkeys or CAPTCHA responses into the app.

Bypass verification

Two-factor checks, device approval and other security prompts remain under the official launcher and account provider.

Run a cloud vault

Relay does not operate an account-cloud service or upload your saved profiles to an external Relay server.
