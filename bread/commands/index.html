Commands

A full list of account, locker, session, and utility commands supported by Bread.

/login

Login to your Fortnite account.

/logout

Logout from your Fortnite account.

/locker view

View your locker categories.

/locker exclusives

View your exclusive cosmetics.

/gifts sent

View gifts you’ve sent to others.

/gifts received

View gifts you've received.

/xp

View your XP progress for all gamemodes.

/requestaccess

Request access to the Fortnite APIs if you haven't accepted the EULA.

/account permissions

View your Epic Games account permissions.

/account ranked

View your Fortnite ranked stats.

/account change-displayname

Change your Epic Games display name.

/account information

View sensitive Epic Games account information.

/account summary

View your Fortnite account summary.

/session

Check your current Fortnite session details.

/checkban social

Check if your account is socially banned.

/checkban account

Check if your Fortnite account is banned.

/creatorlookup

Look up Fortnite Creator profiles.

/estimatedvalue

Estimate your locker’s total value.

/friends acceptall

Accept all pending friend requests.

/friends list

List all Epic Games friends.

/friends removeall

Remove all friends from your account.

/friends search

Search for a user in your friends list.

/packoffer

Fetch Fortnite pack offer data.

/playtime

View your Fortnite PC playtime.

/ranked-modes

Check active Fortnite ranked tracks.

/redeem

Redeem an Epic Games code.

/pendingrewards

Process pending rewards.

/island

Search for a Fortnite Island code.

/vbucks balance

Check your current V-Bucks balance.

/vbucks totalpurchased

View your total V-Bucks purchased.

/status

Fortnite downtime & uptime status.
