Bread Bot

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 05/05/2025
Last Updated: 20/12/2025

Bread Bot ("we", "us", or "our") is a Discord bot designed to provide Fortnite-related functionality, including account management, cosmetic viewing, playtime statistics, and informational updates. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Bread Bot, we may collect the following information:

  • Discord Information
    • Your Discord User ID (used to associate data with your account)
  • Epic Games Authentication Data
    • Device ID
    • Account ID
    • Device secret
    • Display Name

    This data is required to authenticate your Fortnite account using Epic Games' official device authentication system.

  • Authorization codes submitted through Discord. These are only used when strictly necessary and are not retained beyond the authentication process.

2. Platform Authentication (Xbox & PlayStation)

Bread Bot provides optional Xbox and PlayStation login functionality for the playtime command.

  • Authentication is performed using official Microsoft and Sony OAuth flows.
  • Access tokens are generated only for the duration of the requested command.
  • No Xbox or PlayStation credentials, access tokens, or refresh tokens are stored.
  • All platform authentication data is discarded immediately after use.

Bread Bot does not maintain persistent sessions, background refresh jobs, or long-term access to Xbox or PlayStation accounts.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate Fortnite accounts through Epic Games
  • Allow multiple Fortnite accounts to be linked to a single Discord user
  • Provide requested Fortnite-related features and statistics

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

4. Fortnite Pre-Release / Unreleased Content

Bread Bot may display information about unreleased or pre-release Fortnite content ("leaks") that is accessible through Epic Games' publicly available endpoints.

  • Cosmetics not yet available in-game
  • Jam Track data
  • Shop or pass assets marked for future use

This information is collected passively and is provided strictly for informational purposes.

5. Notification Preferences

You may enable or disable notifications for specific update categories, including:

  • Jam Track updates
  • Item Shop updates
  • Pass updates
  • Epic services updates

Notification preferences are saved per Discord server and are fully opt-in.

6. Data Security

User data is stored using reasonable security measures, including encryption and access controls. While we take steps to protect stored data, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Epic Games device authentication data is retained until you use the /logout command.
  • Xbox and PlayStation authentication data is never retained.
  • You may request manual deletion of all associated data at any time.

8. Children’s Privacy

Bread Bot is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used the bot, please contact us so their data can be removed.

9. Contact

10. Data Usage Transparency

“Do not share authentication codes or tokens with third-party services. These credentials allow access to your account.”

Bread Bot only uses authentication credentials to perform actions explicitly requested by the user. Credentials are never reused, shared, or accessed outside the scope of the bot’s functionality.
