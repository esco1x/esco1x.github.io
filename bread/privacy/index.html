Bread Bot is a Discord bot for Fortnite-related tools and optional Steam account features. This policy describes what we collect, why we need it, and how you can remove it.

1. Information we collect

Depending on which commands you use, we may process:

Discord

  • Your Discord user ID (to link stored data to your account)
  • Server and channel IDs when you run commands or enable notifications there

Epic Games (Fortnite)

  • Device ID, account ID, device secret, and display name from Epic’s device authentication flow
  • Authorization codes you submit in Discord, only for completing login and not kept after that step finishes

Steam (optional)

Steam features are opt-in. You link an account through /steam login. If you do, we may store:

  • Steam account name and SteamID64 (when available after login)
  • Encrypted session tokens (refresh and/or access), depending on whether you choose “Remember”
  • Session type (persistent remembered login vs one-off session)
  • Display name and avatar URL for the linked account, when returned by Steam

We do not store your Steam password. Login uses Valve’s authentication flow (including Steam Guard when required).

Xbox and PlayStation (Fortnite playtime only)

  • OAuth is used only to run the Fortnite playtime command
  • Platform tokens are not stored; they are discarded when the command finishes

2. How Steam data is used

When you use /steam subcommands, the bot may request data from Steam on your behalf, including:

  • Profile summary (name, avatar, online status, visibility)
  • Friends list and basic friend presence
  • Owned games and playtime (including recent playtime where visible)
  • Per-game breakdown for a specific app ID: playtime, live status, achievements, and game stats

Most of this comes from Valve’s public Web APIs using a server-side API key. If your profile or game stats are private, some fields may be empty or unavailable. For linked accounts with a valid remembered session, the bot may also request your own stats and achievements through an authenticated Steam client connection when the public API cannot return them.

Your Steam privacy settings matter. Game details, friend lists, and game stats must be visible to Steam (and often set to Public) for the bot to show them. We cannot override Valve’s privacy rules.

Bread Bot is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Valve or Steam.

3. How we use your data

Data is used only to:

  • Run commands you request in Discord
  • Keep your linked Fortnite and/or Steam accounts associated with your Discord user
  • Send opt-in server notifications (Jam Tracks, item shop, passes, Epic services, etc.)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.

4. Fortnite pre-release content

The bot may show unreleased or upcoming Fortnite cosmetics and related metadata from Epic’s public endpoints. This is passive collection for display only, not data you provide.

5. Notifications

Notification settings are per Discord server and opt-in. You can turn categories on or off (for example Jam Tracks, item shop, passes, Epic services).

6. Security

Sensitive tokens are stored encrypted where applicable. No system is perfectly secure; use the bot at your own discretion and do not share login codes or tokens with anyone.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Epic device auth data is removed when you use Fortnite /logout (or equivalent logout flow).
  • Steam session data is removed when you use /steam logout for that account or all accounts.
  • Xbox and PlayStation auth data from playtime commands is never retained.
  • You may ask for manual deletion of your stored data by contacting us (see below).

8. Children’s privacy

The bot is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has linked an account, contact us and we will delete associated data.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: @eggrial on Discord or the support server.

10. Credentials and third parties

Do not paste Steam Guard codes, Epic codes, or session tokens into other services. Bread Bot only uses credentials to perform actions you trigger in Discord.

Third-party services involved may include Discord, Epic Games, Valve/Steam, Microsoft, and Sony, each under their own terms and privacy policies.
